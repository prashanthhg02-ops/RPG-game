# RPG-game
# Emberfall

A small, self-contained browser RPG. Explore Willowmead, help Elder Rowan, and battle forest slimes.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern web browser. No installation, server, or external assets are required.

## Controls

- **Move:** `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` or the arrow keys
- **Talk:** `E` when near Elder Rowan
- **Battle:** choose an action with `1`–`4`, or click its button
  - `1` — Attack
  - `2` — Firebolt (costs 3 mana)
  - `3` — Use a potion
  - `4` — Flee

## How to play

Find Elder Rowan near the village well and talk to him to accept the request. Head east into the Whispering Grove and defeat three slimes. Battles award gold and experience; level-ups increase your health and mana. Potions restore health and mana.
